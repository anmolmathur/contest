# TLE AIdeathon 2026: 10-minute demo walkthrough

A click path for explaining the Ideathon format with the demo contest
(`/c/tle-aideathon-2026-demo`). Everything in the demo is fictional.

**Set-up:** seed the demo in the state you want to show:

- `--state=pitch`: Idea Pitch Friday (crews formed, pitches in, some jury decisions made)
- `--state=live`: mid live run (baselines validated, first actuals coming in)
- `--state=final` (default): finale done, with ranking, awards and value unlocked

## 1. The idea (1 min): landing page
- The **AIdeathon** wordmark: *AI* + *Idea* + *thon*.
- **Value Lenses**: Revenue, Cost, Process, Safeguard, Adoption Unlock, Experience, Decision Intelligence. Tech is a business lever, not a support function.
- **Stages**: Idea Pitch (go/no-go gate) → Blueprint → Build → Live Run → Reveal.
- **Prizes**: ₹1L / ₹60K / ₹40K plus six ₹15K category awards.
- **Value Unlocked meter**: the only numbers that count are ones an Auditor validated and Finance valued.

## 2. Problems worth fixing (1 min): Problem Bank
- Every problem is an outcome, "from X to Y" (admission TAT, RNR at a university…).
- Crews can propose their own. One demo proposal was approved and another rejected (too big for five weeks).
- Known system bugs stay out; they belong in the tech backlog.

## 3. A crew's journey (2 min): log in as demo.participant
- **Your next action**: what to do this week, from the playbook.
- **Crew checklist**: every Heist role covered (one person can play several), crew size, at least 2 departments. The crew can't submit until the checklist is green.
- **Before → After**: the metric, baseline and evidence. The status goes Draft → Waiting for Auditor → Validated.
- **Entries**: the solution paths decide what is required. For example, Agent Build requires an Agent Charter and Tool Purchase requires a business case.

## 4. The gate (1 min): log in as demo.jury, open Scoring on an Idea Pitch
- The Idea Pitch has its own rubric: Expected Value to TLE 30%, Problem 20%, Idea fit 20%, Measurability 10%, Feasibility 10%, Tech & AI angle 10%.
- **Go / Go-with-changes / Out**. The agreed changes follow the crew into every later stage, and Out crews leave the ranking.

## 5. No proof, no points (2 min): log in as the Auditors
- **Data Auditor desk**: check the baseline or actual against the source system and evidence, then validate it or send it back with a note.
- **Finance**: realised ₹ in the live run plus recurring ₹/yr (up to 3 years), with the basis written down.
- If a crew edits a validated number, it goes back to draft automatically.

## 6. Judging the result (1 min): jury scoring on a later stage
- The scoring window shows the validated before/after and the ₹ value.
- The later-stage rubric: Value to TLE 35%, Adoption & Sustainability 25%, AI & Tech Solution 20%, Safety 10%, Reuse 10%.

## 7. The outcome (1 min): Results + Agent Registry
- Podium (Ring Masters, Fast Lane, Exam Autopilot), category awards, and the crews that went out at the gate.
- **Value Unlocked**: total validated value, how many crews have proof, and the mix of solution paths.
- **Agent Registry**: every agent built, its autonomy level (L1–L4), human checkpoint, data and monthly cost. It becomes our agent inventory after the contest.

## 8. Everyone knows their job (1 min): Playbook
- One tab per stakeholder: Participants, Jury, Auditors, Tech Buddies, Sponsors, Organisers. Each shows when, what, where and the output, and highlights "this week".
- PDF per stakeholder (also in this folder).
